ABOUT

Architecture
shaped by people,
place and memory.

I am an architecture graduate based in Melbourne, interested in the relationship between people, landscape and the built environment.

My work explores how architecture can respond to existing conditions rather than simply replacing them. I am particularly interested in memory, materiality, thresholds and the ways people inhabit and adapt to spaces over time.

My architectural education has been shaped by projects across housing, adaptive reuse, education, landscape and infrastructure.

I am interested in architecture as a framework for inhabitation — something that can accommodate change while retaining a connection to the place from which it emerges.
