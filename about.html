About

Chloe Tan is an architecture graduate based in Melbourne, Australia.

Her work explores the relationship between architecture, landscape and inhabitation, with a particular interest in how buildings can respond to their surroundings and change over time.

Her architectural interests extend across housing, adaptive reuse, landscape, materiality and user-centred design.
