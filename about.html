ABOUT

Hi! I'm Chloe, an architecture graduate based in Melbourne.

My name is Chloe, and I am a graduate from the University of Melbourne, having completed my Master of Architecture in 2026 and Bachelor of Design, majoring in Architecture and Construction, in 2023.

My interests involve anything stemming from a creative nature, particularly design and music. I've played the violin since I was seven years old, including travelling overseas on tour, and have always enjoyed engaging with the creative arts.

In my spare time, I love spending time outdoors, travelling, and working on personal projects.

Having completed a major in Construction throughout my undergraduate studies, I have developed an interest in construction processes and materials, as well as the different aspects involved in the built environment industry.

This has led me to pursue work in the infrastructure space. I am currently a Digital Engineering Coordinator in the Design and Engineering Team at John Holland, working on the Suburban Metro Tunnel Project currently under construction in Melbourne's east.
