Turning Point

Project

Turning Point explores the relationship between architecture, landscape and the changing conditions of the forest.

Developed as part of the Architectural Association Visiting School — Forest Futures, the project investigates how architecture can respond to changing ecological conditions while creating new relationships between people and place.

Film

Process

The project developed through a process of mapping, modelling and testing relationships between built form and the surrounding landscape.

The resulting architecture considers the forest not as a backdrop, but as an active condition that informs the organisation and experience of the project.

Drawings

Through drawings and physical models, the project tests how architecture can establish moments of transition between constructed and natural environments.
