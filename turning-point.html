Architectural Association Visiting School (AAVS) / Forest Futures — 2024

Turning Point

Project

Turning Point is a speculative film composed as a diptych, speculating two possible futures for Victoria’s Toolangi State Forest.

The first — speculating on the impact of human exploitation of the forest and its natural resources, through logging and fire management. The second — a scenario where humans shift their forest management perspectives to one that prioritises the health and growth of the forest, allowing for regeneration.

This film has been deeply influenced by the recent politics of forestry management in Victoria, in particular the decision of the state to disband VicForests on June 30 2024. While seen as a step in the right direction, logging still continues today, under the disguise of forest fire management. We believe continued action is required to protect Victoria’s native forests.

For AA Visiting School 2024 — Project Toria.
